## Mock test papers (already on this PC)

14 series of ICAI MTPs from March 2024 to August 2026, copied from `Downloads\New folder` and renamed
`sources/mtp/MTP-<attempt>-S<series>_Q.pdf` / `_A.pdf`.

- These are **third-party copies** of ICAI mock papers: every page carries a watermark
  ("Downloaded from castudyweb.com" or "CA Study Shop"). The questions and answers are ICAI's;
  the watermarks are stripped during extraction and the PDF scanner checks they never reach the book.
- Gaps: **Series II for May 2026 (April 2026) is missing**, and for **Series I September 2026 only the
  answer file exists** — that paper's MCQ text is therefore unavailable, and two SM descriptive
  questions were reconstructed from ICAI's own answers.

## Mapping of file names to attempts

`M` = May, `S` = September, `J` = January, followed by the two-digit year of the exam attempt
(e.g. `MTP-M25-S1` = mock test paper for the May 2025 attempt, Series I, held March 2025).
