customer needs. 
 
To chart its future strategy, Titan Tech conducted a PESTLE analysis. Politically, 
government incentives for digital transformation present growth opportunities, but 
regulatory compliance is tightening. Economically, rising disposable incomes of SMEs 
promise market expansion, yet inflation is driving up operational costs. Technological 
advancements like AI and blockchain are reshaping industry standards, and

increasingly valuing personalized customer support. Lastly, legal changes in data privacy 
regulations pose significant compliance challenges.

investors, identified as high-power, high-interest stakeholders, require constant 
engagement. Employees, although influential, have medium interest, while regulatory
